# Additional Metadata

## Clarified Semantics

- `*_ext` tables are parallel copies of the base table (same `*_id` domain) with near-identical data plus a few extra derived columns (e.g. `races_ext.is_pit_data_available`, `has_sprint`, `max_points`, `short_name`; `constructors_ext.short_name`; `circuits_ext.last_race_year`, `number_of_races`). Do not join an `_ext` table to its base for extra rows; choose one of the pair (`drivers_ext.code`, `lap_times_ext`, `driver_standings_ext` also hold slightly different null/distinct profiles).
- `drives.drive_id` is the ordinal drive slot within a season: `1` = primary/first drive (most common), `2..5` = later stints; `-1` is a sentinel paired with `constructor_id=-1` (non-team/unknown entries, 163 rows). `is_first_drive_of_season`/`is_final_drive_of_season` are explicit flags; `first_round`/`last_round` bound the drive's participation within the season.
- `lap_positions.lap_type` is not all-null as the profile suggests; it classifies each row as `Race`, a `Starting Position - …` category (Grid Drop, Grid Increase, Qualifying, No Qualification, Pit Lane Start), or a `Retirement (…)` type, all keyed by `(race_id, driver_id, lap)`. ~575k rows are pure race laps.
- `lap_times` (PK `race_id, driver_id, lap`) holds only per-lap race data without `lap_type`. `lap_time_stats` is a pre-computed per-driver-per-race summary (avg/stdev in ms and s) covering only a recent subset (531 races, 141 drivers).
- `results.position` is NULL (~10.9k rows) for non-classified finishes; `position_text` (R/D/W/NC/…) and `position_order` are the reliable sort keys; `status_id` (→ `status`) explains why a car retired/failed. `retirements` is a derived view of these nonfinish events with `lap`, `position_order`, `status_id` and a `retirement_type` label (Mechanical Problem / Driver Error / Disqualification).
- `constructor_results.status` is only `'D'` (disqualification, 17 rows) or NULL; it is separate from the drivers' `status` table.
- `qualifying.q1/q2/q3` NULL means the driver did not advance to that session; `position` up to 28. `sprint_results` exists only for the 15 `races_ext.has_sprint=1` events and awards fewer points (`max_points` ≤ 8) than `results`.
- `tdr_overrides` (48 rows) overrides `team_driver_ranks.team_driver_rank` but is keyed by `(year, constructor_ref, driver_ref)` strings, not IDs.
- `short_constructor_names` maps `constructor_ref`→`short_name`; `short_grand_prix_names` maps `races.name` `full_name`→`short_name`. This content is already materialized in `constructors_ext.short_name` and `races_ext.short_name`.
- Coverage is uneven: `results`, standings and `retirements` span essentially all years (1950–2024), but `qualifying` (~482 races), `lap_time_stats` (531), `pit_stops` (273, flagged by `is_pit_data_available`), and `sprint_results`/lap data cover only more recent eras. `races.race_id` goes to 1144 but result/standings/retirement data stop at ~1132.

## Potential Join Strategies

- Race hub: all of laps/pit/qualify/result/standing/retirement/sprint tables join `races`/`races_ext` on `race_id`. Filter to `races_ext.is_pit_data_available=1` before joining `pit_stops`, and to eras whose `race_id` exists in the target table (e.g. restrict `races` to `race_id<=1131` or matching distinct set) to avoid fan-out on missing/empty data.
- Driver/constructor/circuit hub: `driver_id`, `constructor_id`, `circuit_id` (via `races.circuit_id`) connect `drivers`→results/qualifying/pit/lap/standings and `constructors`→results/qualifying/sprint/standings. `results` carries both `driver_id` and `constructor_id`, so a single `results` scan can feed both drivers and constructors, then be aggregated to `constructor_standings`/`driver_standings` on `(race_id, …)`.
- LapData ↔ results: join `lap_times.position`/`fastest_lap` (in `results`, ranked 1..24) and correlate `lap_time_stats`/`lap_times` to `results` on `(race_id, driver_id)`; `pit_stops` likewise on `(race_id, driver_id)` per stop. Watch broad fan-out: `lap_times` is ~575k rows and `lap_positions` ~613k, one labor a lap; aggregate before joining standings.
- Season/drive linking: `drives.account`—join `drives` to `races` on `year` and use `first_round`/`last_round` with `races.round` to restrict to a driver's active races; `drives` also links to `constructors` on `constructor_id` (`-1` sentinel excluded).
- Override chain: `team_driver_ranks` joins `tdr_overrides` on `(year, constructor_ref, driver_ref)` (string refs, not IDs) to apply corrected team driver ranks; fall back to `team_driver_ranks.team_driver_rank` when no override exists.
- Short-name enrichment: join `constructors_ext`/`short_constructor_names` by `constructor_ref` and `races_ext`/`short_grand_prix_names` by `name`/`full_name` to get compact display names; prefer `_ext.short_name` to avoid a table join.
- Status/retirement linkage: `results.status_id`+`retirements` join `status` on `status_id`; `results.position_order` also matches `retirements.position_order` within a race, letting results be reconciled with retirement flags per driver.
